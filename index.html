Personal Project · May 2026 Diagnostic Analytics

Breast Cancer Diagnostic Analysis

SQL-driven exploratory analysis of tumor cell morphology using the UCI Breast Cancer Wisconsin dataset. A project where my biology background and analytics training finally meet — reading these features not just as numbers but as physical properties of cells.

  • 5-script MySQL analysis pipeline covering data cleaning, univariate analysis, feature separation ranking, outlier detection, and variance comparison across 569 patient records
  • Identified concavity mean (3.49x ratio) and concave points mean (3.42x) as strongest diagnostic signals — shape irregularity outperforms size across all feature pairs
  • Found fractal dimension carries zero diagnostic signal (1.00x ratio) — a counterintuitive finding that challenges standard biological assumptions

About

I'm a data analytics professional with an M.S. in Management Science from UT Dallas (Naveen Jindal School of Management, Scholar with Recognition) and a B.S. in Biological Sciences from UC Irvine. My background spans manufacturing analytics, pricing strategy, and market research, with a focus on predictive modeling, structured analysis, and AI tools.

I spent three years at Lelon Electronics in Jiangsu, China — a Taiwanese capacitor manufacturer supplying Bosch, Huawei, and Apple — joining directly out of UC Irvine into an environment I had never worked in, in a language I was still learning, during a pandemic. I built SQL-driven dashboards, ran DMAIC and DOE regression analysis, and reduced defect rates 88% while cutting wastewater costs $7K a month. I led the cross-functional push through Bosch's supplier qualification, resolving 391 compliance gaps to secure a 100,000+ unit purchase order.

At UT Dallas, I took that same instinct into pricing and market strategy. For Texas Instruments, I built a market entry framework across 11 robotics segments and presented it to their executive leadership. For FleetPride, I built an XGBoost and Bayesian model forecasting price elasticity across 5,000+ SKUs, then built an interactive dashboard and a Gemini-powered feature that let sales teams query pricing data directly. I'm especially interested in using AI to build practical tools that turn complex data into something teams can act on quickly.

I'm fluent in English and Mandarin, authorized to work in the U.S., and open to full-time data analyst and data scientist roles.
