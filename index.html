Personal Project · May 2026 Diagnostic Analytics

Breast Cancer Diagnostic Analysis

SQL-driven exploratory analysis of tumor cell morphology using the UCI Breast Cancer Wisconsin dataset. A project where my biology background and analytics training finally meet — reading these features not just as numbers but as physical properties of cells.

  • 5-script MySQL analysis pipeline covering data cleaning, univariate analysis, feature separation ranking, outlier detection, and variance comparison across 569 patient records
  • Identified concavity mean (3.49x ratio) and concave points mean (3.42x) as strongest diagnostic signals — shape irregularity outperforms size across all feature pairs
  • Found fractal dimension carries zero diagnostic signal (1.00x ratio) — a counterintuitive finding that challenges standard biological assumptions

Background

About

I'm a data analytics professional pursuing an M.S. in Management Science at UT Dallas (Naveen Jindal School of Management, Scholar with Recognition). Before grad school, I spent three years at Lelon Electronics in Jiangsu, China — a Taiwanese capacitor manufacturer supplying Bosch, Huawei, and Apple.

I joined directly out of UC Irvine, moving into an environment I had never worked in, in a language I was still learning, during a pandemic. The work was data science in practice before I had the title for it: Python-based workflow analysis, SQL-driven KPI dashboards, iterative A/B testing on polymer formulations. I reduced defect rates 88% through DMAIC and DOE regression modeling, cut wastewater costs $7K/month via SQL anomaly detection, and prevented $500K in contract risk for Huawei, Apple, and Bosch through root cause analysis. I learned that analytical work requires more than a correct answer — it requires one that people can see, trust, and act on.

Now at UTD, I'm applying that same instinct to pricing models and market strategy at scale. I'm fluent in English and Mandarin, authorized to work in the U.S. (STEM OPT eligible, TN visa eligible as a Canadian citizen), and open to full-time data analyst, product analytics, and business analytics roles starting Summer 2026.
